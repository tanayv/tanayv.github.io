Team


Poshak Agrawal, Founder

Poshak studied economics and finance at Princeton University, where he worked with Professor John Mulvey, an international authority in Operations Research and Financial Engineering. He has work experience at TRW, a leading NYSE-listed auto manufacturer, and at Fortress Investment Group, one of world’s largest hedge funds. He served as the Co-President of the Princeton Entrepreneurship Club. Having personally experienced the power of a transformative education, Poshak is deeply passionate about the power of education. He co-founded Athena Education with a mission to help high-school students experience the strength of a holistic education.




Rahul Subramaniam, Director

Rahul Subramaniam will personally guide students through the Career Mentorship program of EDFIN. A native of California, he attended Princeton University, and has mentored hundreds of high-school students over the past several years. With Poshak, he runs Athena’s Personal Mentorship program, India’s leading course for Ivy-League admissions. He enjoys the process of inculcating fundamental skills among students.
