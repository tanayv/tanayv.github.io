The Story

EDFIN was inspired by Athena Education’s own alumni: over the past years, whenever there was a summer or winter break, former Athenians visited us to discuss their college experiences. As we discussed with our former students, we thought deeply about how we could help, how we could systematically enhance the experiences of thousands of Indian undergraduates studying in the US.
