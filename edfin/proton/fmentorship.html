Financial Mentorship

We at Athena believe that an elite education is a privilege, and that students are the engines of their education. The first step to igniting that engine is bearing shared responsibility for financing their education. A college education is a 50-50 joint venture between parents and students: parents undoubtedly have their responsibilities and will meet them, but if a student has a dream, he or she should also contribute equally to it.

Students and parents cover costs together
In other words, students must take responsibility for their own careers and lives, and invest in their own futures. Through this arrangement, students absorb the critical skill of understanding and managing their own financial matters, a value that will remain with them for life.

We offer easy EMIs
We recommend them to split between the parent and student, who will pay after he graduates college. Including the 4 years of an undergraduate education, the total cost of the education can be paid back in installments that are split in 5, 6, 7 or 8 years. It’s always economically viable to take loans in a depreciating currency, as over the years the value of the debt taken will decrease. As seen in the graph, the US Dollar has only become more expensive across the years. Moreover, the student will be earning in USD, while paying back his share of the debt in INR.

EDFIN v Banks

Many banks and other financial institutions do not even provide undergraduate student loans, and if they do, they are highly inconvenient and ill-suited to the needs of families. It is our desire that if parents spend the kind of money they for their children’s education abroad, we want to play our part in facilitating their journey, in as meaningful and hassle - free way possible.

  • Career Mentorship Component
    It recognizes the ultimate purpose of studying in the US is not simply to obtain a US degree, but to use that degree as a springboard to maximize opportunities in career and life.
  • Greatest Convenience
    Clients do not have to run after us for weeks or months, or struggle to follow up, as they might have to do with SBI. Instead, we commit to getting the process started in less than one week. Moreover, for each disbursement, you do not have to keep returning to a branch and waiting in lines; just drop us a message and we will be on the case!
  • Financial Assistance to ALL students
    Conventional banks approve only to 5% of undergraduate students, and really only give loans to postgraduate students. We want to ensure that every student is covered, regardless of family circumstance or need.
