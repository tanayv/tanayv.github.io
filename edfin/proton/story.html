Athena Education

Website

Athena is the Greek goddess of wisdom. At Athena Education, our goal is to help our students become skilled, knowledgeable, and wise. We want them to grow in curiosity, discipline, and maturity. At Athena, our mission is to provide a holistic education. Too many students go through school simply memorizing formulas, without developing the deep understanding necessary to succeed in life and in tests. Even fewer explore important topics like history, philosophy, and art, which are just as necessary as science and engineering.

Our ideal is that of the Platonic Academy, where ancient Greek pupils would develop mastery of all disciplines, from mathematics to philosophy, art to gymnastics. Our Personal Mentorship is a product of this ideal. To help students achieve their dreams of attending iconic universities, we provide test preparation and college counseling, so that our students are optimally prepared for higher education abroad. Success is the contentment that results from knowing you did the absolute best of which you are capable. We hold ourselves and our students to this standard every day.
