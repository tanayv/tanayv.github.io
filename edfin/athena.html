Our goal is to help our students become skilled, knowledgeable, and wise.
We want them to grow in curiosity, discipline, and maturity.


At Athena, our mission is to provide a holistic education. Too many students go through school simply memorizing formulas, without developing the deep understanding necessary to succeed in life and in tests. Even fewer explore important topics like history, philosophy, and art, which are just as necessary as science and engineering.

Our ideal is that of the Platonic Academy, where ancient Greek pupils would develop mastery of all disciplines, from mathematics to philosophy, art to gymnastics. Success is the contentment that results from knowing you did the absolute best of which you are capable. We hold ourselves and our students to this standard every day.



What does Athena do?

To help students achieve their dreams of attending iconic universities, we provide college counseling, so that our students are optimally prepared for higher education abroad.

Athena’s pioneering Personal Mentorship is most powerful training program in India for this purpose. With its all-Ivy-League team, Athena provides guidance not only from knowledge but also from personal experience.


Athena acceptances into top Ivy-League universities are the best in India.
