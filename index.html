Hello, I'm a frontend engineer at PayPal Credit, working on the Global Pay Later team. Previously, I founded Turkbox, a platform to pay for news by classifying data used for training ML algorithm. I'm passionate about building web applications to visualize data, automate tedious tasks for users, and solve problems across varied disciplines. While building applications, I like paying attention to user experience and adopting a product-centric approach.
