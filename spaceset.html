In high school, I was involved with Aeross, the Aerospace Club. We participated in numerous space settlement design competitions, where our objective was to design a space colony of the future.

I worked on Hephaestat, a submission for the 2015 NASA AMES Space Settlement Contest, during my senior year of high school. The project won the Grade 12 First Prize.
